Who’s Certified

Below you will find a searchable list of companies which have earned Certified Humane® certification, and their certified products.

These include farms which raise animals under the Certified Humane® program standards, and companies which produce products from certified animals. These farms and companies are authorized to use the Certified Humane® Raised and Handled trademark on their products which meet the program requirements. Please note that some of the listed companies may also have uncertified products; only the products listed below, and which carry the Certified Humane® logo on the label are certified.

These lists are updated periodically and may not always reflect precise real-time information.
